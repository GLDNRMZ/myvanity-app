Privacy Policy

Last updated November 11, 2026

The short version

Your vanity is private. Without an account, everything stays on your device. With an account, it's backed up so you can use it on other devices. Friends only see the looks you choose to share. We don't sell your data or show ads.

What we store

  • Without an account: your products, looks, photos and settings are stored only in your browser on your device.
  • With an account: your email address, a username, optional display name, bio and profile photo, and a private backup of your vanity (products, looks, wishlist, routines, packing lists, settings and photos).
  • Things you share: looks you share with friends (including their photos and the products in them), hearts and comments.
  • Friends: who you've added, requested, or blocked.
  • Reminders (optional): if you turn on notifications, a push address for your device and the reminder schedule (product names and dates).

Who can see it

  • Your private backup can only be read by you.
  • Shared looks are visible to people you've accepted as friends, unless you set them back to private. Blocking someone removes their access.
  • Your username, display name, bio and profile photo can be seen by signed-in users so friends can find you.
  • Photos are stored at long, unguessable web addresses that only appear to people allowed to see them.

Services we use

  • Supabase (United States): accounts, database and photo storage.
  • Cloudflare: looking up products by barcode or name, and sending reminder notifications.
  • Open Beauty Facts and UPCitemdb: product information when you scan or search. Only the barcode or search words are sent.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting the app's web pages.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, backup, photos, shared looks, comments and friendships. Without an account, Delete all my data clears everything from your device.

Children

MyVanity is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we'll tell you in the app.
